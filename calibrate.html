Calibration

Every rating on the ladder is a label somebody typed. This plays each bot a gauntlet against Stockfish at a known UCI_Elo and reports what it is actually worth.

Run

Games are split evenly between colours and start from a short random opening ply so the same game isn't played twice. Stockfish anchors at 1320 (its own floor), 1600, 1900 and 2200; the bot's rating is fitted from its score against each anchor it actually got a mixed result from.
